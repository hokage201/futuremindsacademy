Questions

Before you ask

Do I need to know Make, Zapier or n8n?

No. We build it and show you how to check it. You only need to know what should happen and when.

Whose accounts do you use?

Yours. Everything is built in your own accounts, so you keep full ownership when we're done.

How much time will this save me?

It depends on your volume, so we don't promise a number. Before we build, we note how long the manual steps take today, so you can compare after.

What if something breaks later?

You get 14 days of fixes after handover. After that, we can help on a small hourly basis or you can use the guide to adjust it yourself.
